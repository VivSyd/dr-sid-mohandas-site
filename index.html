About

Dr Sid Mohandas

Dr Sid Mohandas is a Montessori practitioner and childhood studies scholar whose research in early childhood draws on feminist, decolonial, anticolonial and more-than-human approaches. They are based in London, UK.

Sid's work investigates how gender is constructed and materialised, particularly within Montessori settings, drawing on feminist new materialisms, posthumanism, decolonial and anticolonial theory, and Dalit feminisms. Their research attends to how ordinary objects, spaces and everyday encounters in nursery life carry and produce gendered, racialised, classed and casted forces.

Sid serves as an editorial board member for Reconceptualizing Educational Research Methodology, and is co-editor of a forthcoming special issue in the British Educational Research Journal on the state of gender(s), young people and education.
